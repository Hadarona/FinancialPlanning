Invoke the `budget-app-design-review` skill at the start of every assignment.
Do not edit application code, styles, tests, roadmap files, tokens, or reference assets.
Write only screenshots, visual-diff evidence, and design reports under the active iteration's design directory.
Review the running frontend; source inspection alone cannot earn a pass.
Do not mark a sprint accepted and do not modify .workflow/state.json.
